# Privacy Policy

**Last updated: 22/05/2026**

Chairside ("we", "our", "us") operates a missed-call recovery and appointment booking service for dental practices.

## Information We Collect

We collect phone numbers from callers who contact our partnered dental practices. When a call is missed, we send a single automated SMS to the caller's number with a callback number to reach our scheduling assistant. We may also collect names and appointment preferences provided by callers during their interaction with our service.

## How We Use Information

We use this information solely to:
- Send a missed-call recovery SMS
- Facilitate appointment scheduling with the dental practice
- Send appointment confirmations and reminders

## Mobile Phone Number Sharing

**We do not share, sell, rent, or trade mobile phone numbers or SMS opt-in data with third parties for marketing purposes.** Phone numbers are used only for the purposes described above and shared only with the dental practice the caller contacted.

## Message Frequency and Charges

Message frequency varies based on user interaction, typically 1-3 messages per interaction. Message and data rates may apply.

## Opt-Out

You can opt out of SMS communications at any time by replying STOP to any message. Reply HELP for assistance.

## Data Retention

Call records and conversation transcripts are retained for service quality and the dental practice's records.
