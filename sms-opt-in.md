# SMS Communication Notice

**Last updated: May 29, 2026**

## How patients receive SMS from Chairside-partnered dental practices

Patients of dental practices that use the **Chairside** missed-call recovery service may receive SMS messages from the practice in the following situations:

### Caller-Initiated SMS (Primary Use Case)

When a patient calls their dental practice and the call is not answered by the front desk (e.g., the line is busy or the office is closed), Chairside automatically sends ONE SMS to the caller's phone number on behalf of the practice. The SMS provides a callback number to reach an AI-powered scheduling assistant that can help the patient book an appointment.

**Sample message:**

> "Hi! This is [Practice Name]'s scheduling assistant via Chairside. Sorry we missed your call. Please call us back at [callback number] to book your appointment. Reply HELP for help, STOP to opt out. Msg & data rates may apply."

### Keyword Opt-In (Secondary)

Patients may also explicitly opt in by texting **START** to a Chairside-managed number. This triggers a confirmation SMS:

> "Hi! This is [Practice Name]'s scheduling assistant via Chairside. You're now opted in to receive SMS for appointment booking and confirmations. Reply HELP for help, STOP to opt out. Msg & data rates may apply."

### Appointment Confirmations

Once an appointment is booked, the patient receives a confirmation SMS:

> "You're booked at [Practice Name] for [date/time]. Reply STOP to opt out."

## Consent

By calling a Chairside-partnered dental practice, the patient initiates the contact and consent is implied for a single response SMS confirming next steps. Further messages occur only as part of an appointment booking interaction that the patient continues by calling the callback number.

## Opt-Out

Reply STOP to any message to opt out at any time.

## Frequency and Charges

Message frequency: 1-3 messages per interaction, typically.

Message and data rates may apply per your mobile carrier.
